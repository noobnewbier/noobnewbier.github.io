After Mars Horizon 2 and some internal tooling, 
I joined the [Warhammer Survivor](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3669620/Warhammer_Survivors/) team and release a demo to support our marketing beat. 
The demo have reached ~2700 concurrent players, and has driven thousands of wishlist per day.  

{{< youtube id=ytuRQAHpnbA start=559 >}}

Personal highlight for the demo is seeing some of the highest playtime for any game I have ever seen - and for a demo no less!
It's lovely to see how engaged the players are.

On the technical side, the demo was very stable with no significant bugs,
which I am quite content with as well as I was getting very nervous on release thinking everything is going to blow up.

It is a surprisingly difficult project, turns out trying to display a ton of visual effects on low-end devices requires weird optimization,
and working with 2 major IP holders there are often unexpected curveballs that we needed to dodge! 

On top of the project itself, we also have to face 3 round of redundancies, a corporate acquisition and a change of studio director. 
The success of the title is a testament to the team's resilience and dedication in a very challenging time.   

Highlights:
- Key programmer for demo releases. Liaise with QA and Design to create a successful and stable build. 
- Creating tools for designer to analyze data and balance the game.
- Gameplay programming and bug squashing.
