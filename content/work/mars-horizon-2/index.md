During my time with [Auroch Digital](https://www.aurochdigital.com/), I am the tool programmer for [Mars Horizon 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2281620/Mars_Horizon_2_The_Search_for_Life). 
Whilst Mars Horizon 2 is still work in progress, the tools I created contributed heavily into the creation of our prototypes, and facilitated the release of the official trailer.
   
The announcement was a success, as we can observe in the Mars Horizon Discord, with many supporters of the prequel expressing their anticipation.

I am in charge of technical and feature design of most of our in-house tooling, from cinematic support for artists, data editor for designers,
code generation for programmers and so on. In the process I am responsible from the very beginning, liaising with stakeholders, communicating timelines, to long term support and implementation.   

I also pitched in for gameplay programming when the team need an extra pair of hand on deck.
